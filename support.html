怎樣設為手機桌布？

在大圖按「下載」，桌布原圖會存入「相片」。打開「相片」選這張桌布，按左下角「分享」→「用作背景圖片」，調整後按「加入」即可設為鎖定畫面或主畫面。

甚麼時候有新信封？

每天零時（你 iPhone 的時區）送上一個新信封，每個信封有 10 張桌布。可在「設定」開啟每日提醒。

換手機或刪除 App 後，收藏會否保留？

收藏紀錄只保存在你的 iPhone，刪除 App 或換手機後未必可以恢復。請把喜愛的桌布下載到「相片」作備份。

How do I set a wallpaper?

Tap Download in the full-screen view to save the original to Photos. In Photos, choose it, tap Share → Use as Wallpaper, adjust it and tap Add.

When does a new envelope arrive?

Every midnight in your iPhone's time zone, with 10 wallpapers. You can turn on a daily reminder in Settings.

Will my collection survive a new phone?

Your collection is stored only on your iPhone and may not be recoverable after deleting the app or changing phones. Save the wallpapers you love to Photos.
