《Ella Muse》私隱政策

應用程式：《Ella Muse》（iPhone）｜Bundle ID：com.ellavirtualjourneys.app
適用版本：1.0 或以上｜最後更新：2026 年 11 月 25 日｜English｜使用條款

《Ella Muse》每天送上一個信封，內有 10 張 Ella 的直式手機桌布。Ella 是由 AI 生成的虛構人物。本程式以一次性價格在 App Store 出售，另可選購消耗型 App 內購買「快速領包」；沒有訂閱、廣告或帳號。

一、重點

  • 本程式不設帳戶或登入，不含廣告、第三方分析、追蹤或當機回報 SDK，不使用廣告識別碼，亦不會追蹤你。
  • 你的收藏、最愛、設定及粉絲等級只存放在你的 iPhone，不會傳送給開發者。
  • 為了確認這份 App 是從 App Store 正當取得，並防止圖片被大量下載，開發者的圖片服務只會處理一個由 Apple 購買憑證計算出來的匿名裝置編碼，以及每日下載次數（見第三節）。這些資料不包含你的姓名、電郵、電話、Apple 帳號或付款資料，不會用於廣告或追蹤，亦不能用來識別你的身分。

二、只存放於你裝置上的資料

為提供收藏功能，本程式在本程式的私人儲存空間保存：

  • 你拆開的信封及其中的桌布編號、拆封日期、翻開進度；
  • 最愛、已下載及收納箱內的桌布紀錄；
  • 粉絲等級，以及已送出的新手禮包、晉級禮包及成就禮包；
  • 「快速領包」的購買紀錄（例如日期及包數），用於顯示購買紀錄、計算每日購買上限及鑽石粉絲條件；
  • 你同意《使用條款與圖片授權協議》的版本、時間及語言；
  • 設定：語言、相框顯示、背景音樂及音效、每日提醒開關及時間；
  • 一個在安裝時隨機產生的抽卡次序代碼（只在本機用來決定每日信封的圖片次序，不會傳送出去，亦不能用來識別你）；
  • 圖片快取及雲端規則的本機副本（可在「設定」清除快取）。

上述資料不會傳送給開發者或任何伺服器。iPhone 的 iCloud 或電腦備份可能按你的系統設定包括本程式資料；備份由 Apple 系統控制，開發者無法讀取。刪除本程式即會刪除裝置上的上述資料，屆時收藏紀錄未必可以恢復，請把喜愛的桌布下載到「相片」。

三、網絡使用、購買驗證及每日下載上限

  • 下載內容：本程式經 HTTPS，從開發者在 Cloudflare 上設置的圖片服務下載桌布目錄、桌布圖片及粉絲等級規則（包括新版本提示），不使用 cookie。無法連線時，已下載的收藏照常可以瀏覽。
  • 購買驗證：本程式是付費 App。下載圖片前，本程式會把 Apple 為這份 App 副本簽發的購買憑證（AppTransaction）送到開發者的圖片服務，以核實它是從 App Store、TestFlight 或 Apple 審核取得。服務只核對 Apple 的簽名及本程式的識別碼，不會儲存或記錄這份憑證。憑證不包含你的姓名、電郵或付款資料。
  • 匿名裝置編碼：服務會把憑證中 Apple 為每部裝置的這次安裝提供的裝置驗證值，以單向雜湊（SHA-256，取前 32 個字元）計算成匿名裝置編碼，並簽發一張 24 小時有效的通行證。通行證只存於 App 的記憶體，關閉 App 即消失。編碼不能還原為原值，開發者亦不會把它與你的姓名、Apple 帳號或任何其他資料連結。
  • 每日下載上限：為防止圖片被大量下載，服務按日（世界協調時間）為每個編碼記錄預覽圖及原圖的下載次數；超過每日上限時，下載會暫停至翌日。記錄只有日期、編碼、類別（預覽圖或原圖）及次數，不包括 IP 位址、你下載了哪幾張圖、你的收藏或其他資料。
  • 保存期：這些每日計數只為執行下載上限及防止濫用而保存，不會作其他用途。每筆記錄保存 30 日，之後由服務每日自動刪除；伺服器不會長期保存任何與你裝置相關的資料。
  • 技術記錄及服務供應商：開發者的服務只記錄核實成功或失敗的原因等技術事件，不記錄憑證、通行證或個人資料。Cloudflare 作為服務供應商，可能按其私隱政策處理一般網絡連線資料（例如 IP 位址），以傳送內容及保障安全；開發者不會利用這些資料識別你。

四、App Store 私隱標示

本程式在 App Store「App 私隱」申報的資料是「裝置識別碼」（即第三節的匿名裝置編碼）及「產品互動」（即每日下載次數）。兩項都只用於 App 功能，不會連結到你的身分，亦不會用於追蹤。

五、權限

  • 照片（只限加入）：只在你按「下載」時，把桌布原圖加入「相片」。程式不會讀取你的相簿。
  • 通知（可選）：只在你開啟「每日信封提醒」後，由裝置在本機按你設定的時間提醒你；不涉及推送伺服器。
  • 評分邀請：程式可能在適當時候請 iOS 顯示 Apple 的評分視窗；是否顯示由 iOS 決定，評分及評語由 Apple 處理。

本程式不要求位置、相機、麥克風、通訊錄或追蹤權限。

六、購買

本程式及「快速領包」的付款都由 Apple 處理，開發者不會收到你的付款資料、姓名、電郵或 Apple 帳號資料。「快速領包」的購買紀錄只存於你的裝置（見第二節）。

七、兒童

本程式在 App Store 的年齡分級為 16+，並非為兒童而設，亦不會要求任何人向開發者提供個人資料。

八、你的選擇

你可以隨時在「設定」清除圖片快取；刪除本程式即會刪除裝置上的全部資料。匿名裝置編碼不能還原，開發者無法憑它找出你是誰；如對伺服器上的每日計數有任何查詢，請電郵聯絡我們。

Ella Muse — Privacy Policy

App: Ella Muse (iPhone) · Bundle ID com.ellavirtualjourneys.app · Version 1.0 or later · Last updated 25 November 2026 · Terms of Use

Ella Muse sends you an envelope of 10 portrait phone wallpapers of Ella every day. Ella is a fictional character created with AI. The app is sold on the App Store at a one-time price and offers an optional consumable in-app purchase, the Quick Pack. There are no subscriptions, ads or accounts.

1. Summary

  • There is no account or sign-in, and no advertising, third-party analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDK. The app does not use the advertising identifier and does not track you.
  • Your collection, favourites, settings and fan level stay on your iPhone and are never sent to the developer.
  • To confirm that this copy of the app was obtained from the App Store, and to stop pictures being downloaded in bulk, the developer's image service handles only an anonymous device code derived from Apple's proof of purchase, together with daily download counts (see section 3). This data contains no name, email, phone number, Apple Account or payment details, is never used for advertising or tracking, and cannot identify you.

2. Data kept only on your device

  • The envelopes you opened, the wallpaper IDs in them, the dates and your reveal progress;
  • Your favourites, saved wallpapers and the wallpapers in your storage box;
  • Your fan level and the welcome, level-up and achievement packs already given;
  • Your Quick Pack purchase history (such as dates and number of packs), used to show your purchases, the daily purchase limit and the Diamond Fan condition;
  • The version, time and language in which you accepted the Terms of Use & Image License;
  • Settings: language, photo frame, background music and sound effects, daily reminder on/off and time;
  • A random draw-order code created at install time (used on the device only to order your daily pictures; never sent anywhere and cannot identify you);
  • An image cache and a local copy of the remote rules (the cache can be cleared in Settings).

None of this is sent to the developer or any server. iCloud or computer backups may include it, depending on your system settings; backups are controlled by Apple and the developer cannot read them. Deleting the app deletes this data, and your collection may not be recoverable, so save the wallpapers you love to Photos.

3. Network use, purchase verification and the daily download limit

  • Content: over HTTPS the app downloads the wallpaper catalogue, the pictures and the fan-level rules (including new-version notices) from the developer's image service on Cloudflare. No cookies are used. When you are offline, the wallpapers you already have remain viewable.
  • Purchase verification: this is a paid app. Before downloading pictures, the app sends the proof of purchase that Apple signs for this copy of the app (AppTransaction) to the developer's image service, to confirm it came from the App Store, TestFlight or App Review. The service checks only Apple's signature and the app's identifier; it does not store or log the proof. The proof contains no name, email or payment details.
  • Anonymous device code: from the device verification value in the proof (a value Apple provides for each device and installation), the service computes a one-way hash (SHA-256, first 32 characters) as an anonymous device code and issues a token valid for 24 hours. The token is kept only in the app's memory and disappears when the app is closed. The code cannot be turned back into the original value, and the developer never links it to your name, Apple Account or any other data.
  • Daily download limit: to stop pictures being downloaded in bulk, the service records, per day (UTC) and per code, the number of preview and full-size downloads; above the daily limit, downloads pause until the next day. A record holds only the date, the code, the type (preview or full size) and the count. It does not include IP addresses, which pictures you downloaded, your collection or any other data.
  • Retention: these daily counts are kept only to enforce the download limit and prevent abuse, and are never used for anything else. Each record is kept for 30 days and is then deleted automatically by a daily clean-up; the server keeps no long-term data about your device.
  • Technical logs and service providers: the developer's service logs only technical events such as the reason a verification succeeded or failed, never the proof, the token or personal data. Cloudflare, as a service provider, may process ordinary connection data such as IP addresses under its own privacy policy to deliver content and keep the service secure; the developer does not use it to identify you.

4. App Store privacy label

In the App Store "App Privacy" section the app declares "Device ID" (the anonymous device code in section 3) and "Product Interaction" (the daily download counts). Both are used only for app functionality, are not linked to your identity and are not used for tracking.

5. Permissions

  • Photos (add only): used only when you tap Download, to add the original wallpaper to Photos. The app cannot read your library.
  • Notifications (optional): a local daily reminder at the time you choose; no push server is involved.
  • Rating prompt: the app may ask iOS to show Apple's rating dialog; iOS decides whether it appears and Apple handles ratings and reviews.

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or tracking permission.

6. Purchases

Apple processes the payment for the app and for the Quick Pack; the developer receives no payment details, name, email or Apple Account information. Your Quick Pack purchase history is kept only on your device (see section 2).

7. Children

The app is rated 16+ on the App Store, is not directed at children and never asks anyone to provide personal data to the developer.

8. Your choices

You can clear the image cache in Settings at any time; deleting the app deletes all of its data on your device. The anonymous device code cannot be reversed, so the developer cannot tell who you are from it; if you have any question about the daily counts on the server, please email us.

9. Contact and changes

The app is maintained by an individual developer. For privacy or other questions, email kctst0081@gmail.com. Emails are used only to reply and follow up, never for advertising or sale. If this policy changes, this page and the date above will be updated.
