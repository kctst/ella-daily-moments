《Ella 日常時光》私隱政策

應用程式：《Ella 日常時光》Ella Daily Moments（iPhone）｜Bundle ID：com.ellavirtualjourneys.app
適用版本：0.2.0 或以上｜最後更新：2026 年 11 月 17 日｜English｜使用條款

《Ella 日常時光》每天送上一個信封，內有 10 張 Ella 的直式手機桌布。Ella 是由 AI 生成的虛構人物。本程式為一次性購買，沒有訂閱、App 內購買、廣告或帳號。

一、我們不收集個人資料

本程式不設帳戶或登入，不含廣告、第三方分析、追蹤或當機回報 SDK，不使用廣告識別碼，也不會把你的收藏、設定或任何個人資料傳送給開發者。

二、只存放於你裝置上的資料

為提供收藏功能，本程式在本程式的私人儲存空間保存：

  • 你拆開的信封及其中的桌布編號、拆封日期、翻開進度；
  • 最愛及已下載的桌布紀錄；
  • 設定：語言、相框顯示、每日提醒開關及時間；
  • 粉絲等級及已送出的成就禮包；
  • 一個在安裝時隨機產生的抽卡次序代碼（只在本機用來決定每日信封的圖片次序，不會傳送出去，亦不能用來識別你）；
  • 圖片快取及雲端規則的本機副本（可在「設定」清除快取）。

上述資料不會傳送給開發者或任何伺服器。iPhone 的 iCloud 或電腦備份可能按你的系統設定包括本程式資料；備份由 Apple 系統控制，開發者無法讀取。刪除本程式即會刪除裝置上的上述資料，屆時收藏紀錄未必可以恢復，請把喜愛的桌布下載到「相片」。

三、網絡使用：只用於下載桌布及規則

本程式經 HTTPS 從開發者在 Cloudflare 上設置的唯讀圖片服務下載桌布目錄、桌布圖片及粉絲等級規則（包括新版本提示）。這些請求只包含所需檔案的路徑，不附帶任何個人資料、收藏紀錄或裝置識別碼，亦不使用 cookie。開發者的服務不會記錄或分析個別用戶的請求。Cloudflare 作為服務供應商，可能按其私隱政策處理一般網絡連線資料（例如 IP 位址）；開發者不會利用這些資料識別你。無法連線時，已下載的收藏照常可以瀏覽。

四、權限

  • 照片（只限加入）：只在你按「下載」時，把桌布原圖加入「相片」。程式不會讀取你的相簿。
  • 通知（可選）：只在你開啟「每日信封提醒」後，由裝置在本機按你設定的時間提醒你；不涉及推送伺服器。
  • 評分邀請：程式可能在適當時候請 iOS 顯示 Apple 的評分視窗；是否顯示由 iOS 決定，評分及評語由 Apple 處理。

本程式不要求位置、相機、麥克風、通訊錄或追蹤權限。

五、購買

本程式以一次性價格在 App Store 出售。付款由 Apple 處理，開發者不會收到你的付款資料、姓名或 Apple 帳號資料。

六、兒童

本程式並非為兒童而設，亦不會要求任何人向開發者提供個人資料。

七、聯絡及政策變更

Ella Daily Moments — Privacy Policy

App: Ella Daily Moments (iPhone) · Bundle ID com.ellavirtualjourneys.app · Version 0.2.0 or later · Last updated 17 November 2026 · Terms of Use

Ella Daily Moments sends you an envelope of 10 portrait phone wallpapers of Ella every day. Ella is a fictional character created with AI. The app is a one-time purchase with no subscription, in-app purchases, ads or accounts.

1. We do not collect personal data

There is no account or sign-in, and no advertising, third-party analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDK. The app does not use the advertising identifier and never sends your collection, settings or any personal data to the developer.

2. Data kept only on your device

  • The envelopes you opened, the wallpaper IDs in them, the dates and your reveal progress;
  • Your favourites and which wallpapers you saved;
  • Settings: language, photo frame, daily reminder on/off and time;
  • Your fan level and the achievement packs already given;
  • A random draw-order code created at install time (used on the device only to order your daily pictures; never sent anywhere and cannot identify you);
  • An image cache and a local copy of the remote rules (the cache can be cleared in Settings).

None of this is sent to the developer or any server. iCloud or computer backups may include it, depending on your system settings; backups are controlled by Apple and the developer cannot read them. Deleting the app deletes this data, and your collection may not be recoverable, so save the wallpapers you love to Photos.

3. Network use

Over HTTPS the app downloads the wallpaper catalogue, the pictures and the fan-level rules (including new-version notices) from the developer's read-only image service on Cloudflare. Requests contain only the file paths, with no personal data, collection records, device identifiers or cookies, and the developer's service does not log or analyse individual requests. Cloudflare, as a service provider, may process ordinary connection data such as IP addresses under its own privacy policy; the developer does not use it to identify you.

4. Permissions

  • Photos (add only): used only when you tap Download, to add the original wallpaper to Photos. The app cannot read your library.
  • Notifications (optional): a local daily reminder at the time you choose; no push server is involved.
  • Rating prompt: the app may ask iOS to show Apple's rating dialog; iOS decides whether it appears and Apple handles ratings and reviews.

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or tracking permission.

5. Purchase

The app is sold on the App Store at a one-time price. Apple processes the payment; the developer receives no payment details, name or Apple account information.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children and never asks anyone to provide personal data to the developer.

7. Contact and changes

The app is maintained by an individual developer. For privacy or other questions, email kctst0081@gmail.com. Emails are used only to reply and follow up, never for advertising or sale. If this policy changes, this page and the date above will be updated.
